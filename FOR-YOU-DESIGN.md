## Движение

У голубей и лесных птиц — четыре нарисованных положения крыльев с мягким переходом, небольшое движение в полёте и реакция на прокрутку. Кулон слегка качается и меняет блик. Золотая лента строится как объёмная SVG-поверхность вдоль страницы, с авторским знаком. При отключённой анимации или системной настройке уменьшения движения всё остаётся статичным.

## Промпты генерации

### oil-sky

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed luxury fashion identity website background, landscape wide 3:2. Generate a NEW original oil painting backdrop inspired by the REFERENCE IMAGE's expressive real impasto brush texture and olive, sage, aged-gold, warm ivory palette. Only the BACKGROUND, with absolutely NO birds, animals, text, logos or objects: luminous atmospheric sage-olive painted air, deep muted olive shading around the outer corners, soft golden cloudlike brushwork across the upper right and lower left, delicate old-master oil paint strokes, elegant museum-quality painted surface. The center must be calm medium-dark olive with sparse texture so a gold pendant can be placed over it later. Rich painterly tonal depth, restrained warm luminosity, couture elegance. This must look like a genuine painted scene rather than a flat texture swatch or photograph. Fill the entire image edge to edge. No frame, no typography, no digital gradients, no silk, no human faces, no jewelry, NO BIRDS (they are animated separately). Render the largest high-quality landscape output.

### dove-atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: precisely aligned 2x2 animation sprite sheet on a genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha background for a luxury fashion website. Draw ONE ivory dove in the EXACT expressive oil-painted impasto style of the reference, represented in FOUR distinct successive wingbeat poses. Square canvas divided into four equal invisible cells, no visible grid. The SAME dove faces LEFT in every cell, with its head and torso registered at the SAME center coordinates within each cell, same body scale, same anatomy, same lighting, same tail. Top-left: both wings raised in a beautiful high V. Top-right: wings halfway lowered, broad lifted spread. Bottom-left: wings extended almost horizontally. Bottom-right: wings lowered in a graceful downstroke. Keep the head, eye, beak and torso identical between poses; only the wings change. Each entire bird including ALL feathers must fit well inside its own quarter, generous transparent padding at cell boundaries, no overlapping cells. Cream-white feathers, warm ivory paint, olive-gray shadow strokes, tiny natural red-brown feet, visible oil-brush texture. No realistic photographic feathers, no cartoon, no lettering, no props, no cast floor shadows, no background, no marks or labels. FOUR birds only, exactly one per equal quarter. Largest high quality square output, sharp textured strokes.

### golden-grove

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-width website section background painting for an elegant fashion identity. Generate an original enchanted golden grove inspired by the reference: delicate branching trees formed from fine textured champagne gold leaf, warm ivory parchment and misty pale sage background, exquisite engraved and oil-painted quality, tiny elegant twig networks, quietly magical luxurious atmosphere. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Golden trunks and branches mainly in the outer LEFT and RIGHT quarters, with a generous calm warm ivory and pale sage clearing across the central half for readable editorial content. The foreground bottom corners can have slightly stronger gold tree detail. Uniform soft diffuse gallery light. Real fine gold-leaf texture, no glitter noise. NO birds anywhere (separate animated birds will be added in the website), no people, typography, logos, jewelry, borders, watermarks or frame. This should feel like an elegant gold botanical mural, no cartoon forest. Render largest high-quality landscape.

### songbird-atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: precisely aligned 2x2 animation sprite sheet on a genuinely transparent alpha background. ONE small black-and-champagne-gold woodland songbird matching the golden birds in the reference's gilded botanical mural style, represented in FOUR wing-opening poses. Square canvas, four equal invisible cells, no grid. Same bird faces RIGHT in every cell; identical registered head, torso and feet centered within each equal quarter, same size and scale throughout. Top-left wings folded against the body, as if perched on an invisible twig; top-right wings opening halfway; bottom-left wings fully lifted in a graceful open V; bottom-right wings opening outward broadly. Keep the head, torso and feet in precisely the same place between frames. Extremely fine oil-painted and engraved gold-leaf feather details, black head and back, warm pale-gold chest, calm natural anatomy, sophisticated rather than cute. Entire bird including wing tips fully within every cell with transparent margin. Exactly FOUR birds, one per quarter. No branches, no background, no text, no props, no shadows. Largest square high-quality output.

### black-packaging

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: refined landscape 3:2 fashion brand-application still life. Input 1 is the EXACT transparent BLACK FOR YOU wordmark; faithfully preserve its distinctive letterforms and the subtitle for you brand. Input 2 supplies the user's original visual system: small ivory paper bags, muted olive box with gold-tree pattern, linen ribbon and natural materials. Input 3 supplies the black woven-clothing-label style. Create a NEW ultra high quality natural studio still life on a warm ivory and pale muted-sage surface: one SMALL ivory textured-paper shopping bag with fine BLACK fabric handles and the exact BLACK printed logo at realistic modest size; one low muted-olive clothing box with delicate gold-tree illustration on the lid and a small BLACK logo on an ivory panel; two small ivory garment hangtags with fine holes and natural thread, printed with the BLACK logo; a softly curling narrow natural linen ribbon printed in black with the same logo. All objects fully in frame with plentiful breathing room, no enormous closeups, bag occupies at most one third of the image height, believable relative scales. Soft daylight, tactile paper and fabric detail, quiet couture editorial photography, elegant balanced asymmetrical composition. NO gold foil LOGOS on the packaging, all logos BLACK. No people, cards, fake QR codes, extra writing, pigeons, screens, frames or watermark. Preserve the reference wordmark carefully. Conceptual visualization, not an actual merchandise production photo.

### pendant

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: isolated photoreal gold pendant for a luxury brand website hero, genuine TRANSPARENT alpha background. The first input is the user's EXISTING FOR YOU custom-letter gold necklace pendant, a reference for its exact form; the second input is the exact black logo as letterform reference. Create a new immaculate high-resolution isolated render of that very gold FOR YOU letterform pendant, no subtitle, front view with a subtle natural 8-degree turn, polished pale 18k gold with real bevels, warm reflections, subtle directional specular highlights, fine realistic edges. Preserve the original distinctive logo, do not invent a different font or add letters. Include a fine elegant gold chain rising from the two upper attachment points and curving out of the TOP edge, both sides symmetrical enough for a gently hanging pendant; entire gold letterform comfortably within frame. Refined fine-jewelry product photography, natural gold rather than yellow plastic, no exaggerated star sparkles. No doves, no background, no olive plate, no ground, no cast shadow slab, no text other than the exact letterform logo, no watermark. Transparent pixels between and inside letterforms and chain links. Largest high-quality output.
