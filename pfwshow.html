Overview

About
the project

The goal was to create a bold and elegant visual identity that reflects the atmosphere of Paris, high fashion and the unique experience of the event.

"The identity works across digital, print and environmental applications."

The system needed to feel timeless yet modern — rooted in the visual codes of haute couture while remaining versatile across all touchpoints: social media posts, environmental signage, printed certificates and branded merchandise.
